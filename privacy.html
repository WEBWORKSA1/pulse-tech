Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Pulse.Tech ("we", "us") collects when you use pulse.tech, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (newsletter, partner matching, contact, contest, job application, sponsorship or donation pledge) we receive the details you enter, such as name, email, company and message. We use them only to respond to your request, deliver the newsletter you asked for, or — for matching requests — share your brief with relevant partners. We do not sell personal information.

Form processing

Forms are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-relay service, which processes submissions on our behalf.

Cookies and advertising

We use a small amount of browser storage to remember your theme and cookie choice. We display ads served by Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland will be shown a consent message before personalised ads are used.

Embedded content

Video embeds use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you click play. Live data panels request public data from the Hacker News and GitHub APIs directly from your browser.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or unsubscribe at any time, via our contact page (choose "Privacy / data request").

Children

Pulse.Tech is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the date above when we do.
