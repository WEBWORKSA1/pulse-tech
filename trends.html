      How to read these signals

      Stars ≠ adoption

      A spike in GitHub stars shows attention, not production use. Check commit activity, issues and licence before betting on a project.

      Upvotes show debate

      Highly upvoted stories often signal controversy or surprise. Read the comments for practitioner perspective.
