The SaaS Buying Checklist: 20 Questions to Ask Before You Sign

By Pulse.Tech Editorial · 6 min read · Updated November 2026

Twenty questions covering fit, cost, security, data ownership and exit terms to ask any software vendor before purchase.

Software contracts are easy to sign and painful to leave. These twenty questions help you compare vendors on the things that matter after the demo ends.

Fit

  1. Which of our top three workflows does it handle out of the box?
  2. What would we need to customise or integrate?
  3. Can we trial it with our own data?
  4. Which similar companies use it, and can we talk to one?

Cost

  1. What is the total cost in year one, including onboarding and add-ons?
  2. How does price change as we add users or usage?
  3. What is the renewal increase cap?
  4. Are there minimum seat commitments?

Security and compliance

  1. Which independent security audits or certifications do you hold?
  2. Is single sign-on included or extra?
  3. Where is our data stored and backed up?
  4. Is our data used to train AI models?

Data and exit

  1. Can we export all our data in a standard format at any time?
  2. What happens to our data after cancellation?
  3. What is the notice period to cancel?
  4. Is there an API with reasonable limits?

Support and roadmap

  1. What support response times are guaranteed on our plan?
  2. Who is our point of contact after signing?
  3. What has shipped in the last six months?
  4. What is the uptime commitment and how are credits handled?
Tip: send the same questions to every shortlisted vendor in writing. The answers — and how quickly they arrive — tell you a lot.
